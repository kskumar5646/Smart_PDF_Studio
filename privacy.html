Smart PDF Studio Privacy Policy: Your privacy is our absolute priority. All 24 PDF tools process files 100% client-side in your browser - your PDFs, images, and documents NEVER leave your device and are never uploaded to any server. We do not store, track, collect, or share your personal data, files, browsing history, or PDF content. No registration required, no personal information collected. We use minimal local storage only to save your language preference from 11 languages including Hindi, English, Spanish, French, German and theme setting so you dont need to select each time. We do not use tracking cookies, fingerprinting, or personal identifiers. Your files are processed in RAM and automatically cleared when you close tab. We do not have access to your files, we cannot see, read, or copy them. For Google AdSense, Google as third party vendor uses cookies to serve ads. Google use of DART cookie enables it to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. We do not share your PDF content with advertisers. We may collect anonymous usage statistics like page views via Google Analytics without personal identification to improve tools. We comply with GDPR and CCPA. You have right to clear local storage anytime. Childrens privacy - we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Data security - since processing is local, your data is as secure as your device. If you have privacy concerns, email smartpdfstudio2026@gmail.com. This policy updated January 2026.
